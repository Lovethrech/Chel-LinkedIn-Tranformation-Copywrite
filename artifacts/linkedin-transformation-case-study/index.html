The brief

Make expertise
legible.

Maya Reynolds is a fictional leadership coach with a real positioning problem: years of experience, a generous point of view, and a profile that asks visitors to do too much work.

Her original LinkedIn presence reads like a complete career history. It is accurate, but it is not directional. It names the rooms she has been in without making clear who she is best placed to help next.

This concept project tests a simple question: what changes when a profile is written as a pathway rather than a catalogue?
