* What problems she solves

COPYWRITING DECISIONS

Create a section titled:

“Every change has a job.”

Present a table or series of cards with these comparisons:

1. Positioning

Before: “Executive Coach”
After: “Executive Leadership Coach for Newly Promoted Managers”
Reason: Narrows the audience and makes the specialization immediately understandable.

2. Problem awareness

Before: “Helping professionals achieve their goals”
After: “Leading people requires a different set of skills”
Reason: Opens with a specific transition the target reader is likely experiencing.

3. Outcome language

Before: “Leadership coaching and personal development”
After: “Communicate clearly, delegate effectively, and manage conflict”
Reason: Replaces abstract service language with tangible workplace outcomes.

4. Differentiation

Before: “My approach is tailored to each client”
After: “Practical leadership tools, personalized coaching, and real workplace application”
Reason: Explains how the coaching experience is different instead of relying on a common claim.

5. Call to action

Before: “Contact me to learn more”
After: “Send me a message with the word ‘LEAD’”
Reason: Gives interested prospects one simple and memorable next step.
